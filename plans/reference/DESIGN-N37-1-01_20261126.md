## 1. 预注册硬门（`O1..O6`）

| 门 | 名称 | 预注册判据 |
|---|---|---|
| `O1` | `O1_fsm_transition_graph` | 8 态（`Qualified / Trial / Running / Waiting / Shadow / Restricted / Paused / Archived`）严格按 `MAINLINE.md §4.5` Mermaid 图转移；非法转移抛 `OperationalTransitionError` 且不改状态 |

| `O5` | `O5_restricted_guard_and_recovery` | `enter_restricted` 阻断变更（`LifecycleError`）；`recover_from_restricted(health_ok=False)` 转 `Paused`；`recover_from_restricted(health_ok=True)` 核验完整性后恢复 `Running` |
| `O6` | `O6_retirement_archival_verification` | `retire_and_archive(archive_dir)` 结算/导出未结事项、写出归档包并用 `BoundedDeliveryRuntime.open(archive_dir)` 验证 `record_digest` 与 `chain_head` 一致，随后主包永久转 `Archived` 并撤除一切写入与转移权利 |
