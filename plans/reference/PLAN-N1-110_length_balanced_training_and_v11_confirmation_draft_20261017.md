# PLAN-N1-110 · length-balanced material training and v11 confirmation · 2026-10-17

**Status: owner-authorized and frozen on 2026-10-17 under the user's standing instruction to continue the N mainline and flexibly revise plans to match project evidence.** This authorizes the length-balanced training recipe and conditional v11 one-shot confirmation exactly as bounded below; it does not change any gate or authorize capability promotion.

## Evidence and question

The run100 train-derived schedule contains only six-character known `lookup`/`revision` answers (1,536/1,536). The consumed v10 diagnostic crossed six- and seven-character answers. In the position arm, seed11 has one seven-character lookup template that always abstains; seed12 has one seven-character lookup and one revision template with one-character errors; seed13 fails every lookup/revision template at both lengths, with two-to-seven-character edit distance. Stored `teacher_exact` and `free_exact` agree on all 2,232 v10 rows, so these errors also occur under gold-prefix scoring. The length gap may explain part of the seed11/12 pattern but does not explain seed13.

Question: does equal six/seven-character material exposure improve exact answer prediction under the same architecture and update budget, while preserving the original train and confirmation gates?

## Interpretation and limits

A candidate that only improves seven-character items but leaves seed13 broad failures would not satisfy the multi-seed confirmation gate. Training success, range success, and confirmation success remain separate decisions.
